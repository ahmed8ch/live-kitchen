Restaurant Wi-Fi vs. Mobile Data Architecture

What do commercial restaurants actually do regarding Wi-Fi vs. Mobile Data?

1. Zero-Install Mobile Data First: Modern dining web apps load in <1.2 seconds on 4G/5G (<450KB payload). In Egypt, >95% of diners have active mobile data.
2. The Quota Abuse Problem in Egypt: Egyptian home/business DSL packages have strict monthly caps (140GB - 300GB). Completely open Wi-Fi gets exhausted in days by video streams and downloads.
3. The Recommended Industry Solution: A captive Wi-Fi SSID with DNS whitelisting allowing only the ordering portal domain and payment gateways, blocking high-bandwidth video domains.
